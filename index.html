What Mark Does

Electrical Services Done Properly

From whole-house renovations to a single safety switch — done to Australian Standards, every time.

Home Renovation Wiring

From a single room refresh to a whole-house renovation, Mark works alongside you across the project to plan power points, lighting and circuits properly the first time. He'll flag smarter, more efficient options before the plaster goes up.

Switchboard Upgrades & Assessments

Pre-purchase switchboard checks and full upgrades to bring older Ballarat homes up to current Australian Standards. You'll get a clear written assessment so you know exactly what you're buying or what needs replacing.

Power Points & Rewiring

New GPOs, dedicated circuits for ovens and air-cons, and full or partial rewiring of older weatherboards and brick veneers around the region. Tidy work, cable runs done right, and no holes left where they shouldn't be.

LED Lighting & Downlights

Indoor and outdoor LED lighting designed to actually suit how you use the room, not just dotted across the ceiling. Expect honest advice on colour temperature, dimming, and where the light will genuinely make a difference.

Safety Inspections & Testing

Safety switch installations, smoke alarm compliance, and full property electrical inspections for landlords and home owners. Everything documented and explained in plain English.

Honest Local Advice

Pick up the phone for a no-pressure chat. Mark's been at this long enough to know when something needs doing, and when it can wait. Straight answers, every time.
